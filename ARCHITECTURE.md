# Architecture and contracts

**Status:** draft, 2026-11-03. Solid arrows denote proposed data contracts, not existing software; tests not run.

`inlet field → sector reduction → compressor map response → common downstream matching → shaft/load balance → result record`

Keep the spatial field and reduction manifest alongside results: a mean-only adapter must not silently erase distortion. The steady system uses prescribed speed and downstream load boundary; its matching equations and unknowns must be fully declared in P0. A future dynamic driver wraps this steady algebraic component, not vice versa.

## Data contracts

All data carry schema version, source and content hash, coordinates, mask, uncertainty where known, units, and processing history. SI is canonical; conversions must be explicit and reversible. Reject NaNs, missing required fields, inconsistent geometry, unsupported fluid and invalid values; never fill physical data by default.

**Inlet:** annular station and plane normal; right-handed stationary cylindrical `(x,r,θ)` with θ origin, orientation, rotation sense, hub/shroud and cell area. Preserve grid/cell bounds, total pressure `Pt` Pa, total temperature `Tt` K, and velocity components `(Vx,Vr,Vθ)` m/s or an explicitly identified equivalent, plus static pressure/temperature when supplied. Label total versus static and absolute versus relative frame; transform relative velocity with `V_abs = V_rel + Ω×r`, with Ω in rad/s. Swirl angle is `atan2(Vθ,Vx)` with sign convention; reject undefined flow. Distinguish time instant, phase average and time mean. State area, mass-flux or other weighting, formula, mask and missing coverage; do not substitute one average for another. Preserve raw arrays and projection error. The initial approximation accepts stationary, radially uniform `Pt(θ)`, uniform `Tt`, positive axial flow and zero swirl within frozen tolerances. Explicitly reject unsupported inlet swirl, radial variation or requested radial redistribution; no silent homogenization or claim to model redistribution.

**Map:** source/version/rights, machine and geometry, valid speed lines, corrected flow and speed conventions, pressure-ratio orientation, total-to-total efficiency and interpolation method. For declared ideal-gas air, use `θ=Tt/Tref`, `δ=Pt/Pref`, `Nc=N/√θ`, `Wc=ṁ√θ/δ`; store `N` in rad/s, `ṁ` kg/s, `Tref` K and `Pref` Pa. Define whether map uses dimensional corrected values or a separately specified nondimensional convention; never mix them. Store fluid composition, gas constant, heat capacity or property-model identity/version and range. Map cell/line limits are data limits, not stability limits; no extrapolation. Revisit constant-property air if measured temperatures or fluid demand a validated property model.

**Coupling:** per sector input comprises inlet totals, area, speed and load boundary. Outputs include `ṁ` kg/s, discharge total enthalpy J/kg and total pressure Pa, fluid power W and resisting torque N·m. For no bleed or heat transfer, `Σṁin=Σṁout`, `Pfluid=Σṁ(h_t,out−h_t,in)`, and at nonzero speed `τresist=Pfluid/Ω` with losses separately declared. A steady imposed-speed drive supplies equal opposing torque; avoid division at zero speed. Do not claim thrust from these interfaces.

## Proposed approximation and decisions

Use stationary sectors covering the annulus with `α_i=A_i/A_annulus > 0` and `Σα_i=1`; equal-angle sectors are a convenient special case. **Assumption:** the whole-annulus map, evaluated at sector-local inlet totals, common shaft speed and the candidate discharge total pressure, returns a *whole-annulus-equivalent* local mass flow `ṁeq_i`. Convert it once to sector flow `ṁ_i=α_i ṁeq_i`; neither pressure ratio nor efficiency is multiplied by `α_i`. Compute sector fluid power as `ṁ_i (h_t,out,i−h_t,in,i)`, sum it once, and derive resisting torque once as `Pfluid/Ω` for nonzero speed. Do not sum unweighted map flows or weight the resulting total a second time. Match the summed flow to the declared downstream load. Freeze root-selection/branch policy; reject multiple roots or missing map support. Duplicating a sector with identical conditions and weights summing to its original weight must leave flow, power and torque unchanged.

Common **discharge total pressure** is a candidate surrogate closure, not established physical matching truth or static-pressure compatibility. This parallel-sector idealization excludes inter-sector mixing, upstream swirl and radial flow redistribution, swirl generation and rotor unsteadiness. Before any predictive claim, a qualified domain reviewer must assess a documented physical basis or a comparison against an appropriate alternate closure, matched conditions and uncertainty; an unavailable or inconclusive review blocks the claim. Even a favorable closure review is not experimental validation. Spatial location is retained, but this model cannot infer circumferential interaction or phase-sensitive response.

Propose a small independent, versioned core with adapter boundaries rather than require a specific framework. Compare [pyCycle](EVIDENCE.md), [T-MATS](EVIDENCE.md) and [FullFlow](EVIDENCE.md) for cycle/network reuse; revisit if a verified integration meets contracts with less maintenance and acceptable license/runtime dependencies. `turbo-design` is a radial-equilibrium design alternative, AGTF30 an engine-system reference, and SU2 a CFD alternative, not interchangeable validation truth. Investigate actual versions and rights before any adapter; README claims are not maturity assessments.

**Dynamic proposal only:** states include shaft angular speed and, only if modelled, plenum stored mass/internal energy; algebraic variables include matched flows and pressures. `J dΩ/dt=τdrive−τresist−τloss`; pressure storage requires a consistent equation of state and energy balance. Initialize at a verified steady equilibrium; bounds and events stop integration on map/envelope exit. Failed trial steps restore all states, time and history. Lack of transient measurements blocks physical transient validation, not necessarily numerical verification.
